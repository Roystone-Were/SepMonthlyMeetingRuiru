05 · Issues

Issues raised.

  1. Marketing and operational updates need to be communicated consistently to the wider team.
  2. Robert and the relevant marketing lead were tasked with ensuring new information is included in morning/afternoon briefings.
  3. An emergency exit for the shop to be identified.
  4. The branch raised concerns around system speed, efficiency and customer information.
  5. System improvements should support customer classification, benefits, points and delivery information.
  6. The generator in Ruiru isn’t operational at the moment.

07 · People

HR updates.

The following key HR matters were highlighted:

  1. Attendance and punctuality: staff were reminded to observe working hours and report to work on time.
  2. Leave management: employees were encouraged to follow the proper leave application procedures and communicate their requests through the appropriate channels.
  3. Staff conduct: emphasis was placed on adhering to company policies, procedures, and professional standards.
  4. Employee welfare: staff were encouraged to raise workplace concerns through the appropriate channels.
  5. Communication: employees were reminded of the established channels for reporting staff issues and seeking assistance.
  6. Staff activities: upcoming staff activities and important HR dates were highlighted.
08 · People

Staff welfare.

The meeting discussed the importance of maintaining a healthy and supportive working environment. Key points included:

  1. Promoting mutual respect and understanding among colleagues.
  2. Encouraging staff to communicate challenges that may affect their well-being or work performance.
  3. Supporting colleagues experiencing difficult personal circumstances.
  4. Encouraging participation in staff welfare initiatives.
  5. Maintaining confidentiality and sensitivity when handling personal staff matters.
09 · Customers

Performance and customer experience.

The meeting emphasized the role of every employee in achieving business objectives and delivering an excellent customer experience. The following areas were highlighted:

  1. Providing friendly, professional, and consistent customer service.
  2. Taking ownership of customer concerns and ensuring timely resolution.
  3. Strengthening teamwork and collaboration between departments.
  4. Maintaining professionalism when dealing with customers, colleagues, and management.
  5. Working towards individual, departmental, and branch targets.
  6. Handling customer complaints promptly, respectfully, and effectively.

Staff were encouraged to embrace a customer-focused culture and contribute to the overall success of their respective branches.

The SPOCs around the hub — Githurai, Lumumba and TRM pharmacies — are doing well, with Githurai sales coming up. We are looking forward to having them break even too.
